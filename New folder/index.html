Freelancer Fee & Tax Guide (India & Global)

What is Hidden FX Spread?

When receiving foreign currency payments, payment providers convert funds using a rate 2%–4% lower than the real Google mid-market exchange rate. That hidden gap is an FX spread fee.

How is Section 194-O TDS Calculated?

For Indian remote workers, e-commerce & freelance platforms like Upwork deduct 1% TDS on gross payouts as mandated under Section 194-O. You can claim this back when filing your ITR.
