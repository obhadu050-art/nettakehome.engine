3 Things That Change Your Real In-Hand Salary Most

  • Variable Pay / Performance Bonus: A ₹20 LPA offer with 15% variable pay gives lower regular monthly pay than ₹20 LPA fixed.
  • Employer PF Part of CTC: Many companies include both employee and employer PF inside CTC, which reduces monthly take-home compared to gross pay.
  • Tax Regime & Deductions: Standard deduction, NPS, or exemptions can shift monthly tax outgo noticeably.
